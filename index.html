First time on a party boat?

  1. Reserve a spot online (offshore trips must be booked ahead).
  2. Arrive 1 to 1.5 hours early. Boarding goes in reservation order.
  3. Bring a cooler, ice, food, layers and cash for the mate's tip and fish cleaning.
  4. Fish. The crew helps with rigs, tangles and gaffing.

Know before you go

What should I bring?

A cooler up to 100 qt for your catch, a small one for food and drinks, ice (sold at the dock and tackle shop), layers and foul-weather gear. Bring your own rod and tackle or rent on board.

Bring cash. Seriously.

Tips, fish cleaning, ice and the snack bar are cash only and there is no ATM nearby.

How much should I tip the mate?

Tip on how helpful the crew was, not how many fish you caught. 15 to 20% of your fare is typical.

Can you clean my fish?

Yes. The crew cleans, fillets and bags your catch for a small fee.

Food and drinks?

Snack bar with chips, sodas, water and microwavable food. Coolers welcome. Cans only, no glass. No hard alcohol; beer limited to one six-pack per adult.

When do I board?

Day trips board up to 1.5 hours early, offshore trips 1 hour early, in reservation order.

What if the weather is bad?

The boat sails in the rain. Only the captain cancels, and you'll get a call and email if so. Don't assume a cancellation.

Anything not allowed?

GPS and tracking devices are prohibited. On tuna trips, braid is for jigging only; if you bait-fish with braid you need a 200-yard mono top-shot.
